Choose the membership level that fits your mission

Whether your organisation builds on enterprise Java, contributes to its evolution, or leads its strategic direction, Jakarta EE offers membership levels designed to match your goals and level of engagement.

Each tier provides unique opportunities for influence, visibility, and participation in shaping the future of open, cloud native enterprise Java.

Strategic

For organisations where enterprise Java is strategic and who want to shape Jakarta EE’s long-term direction.

$25k – $300k USD
based on annual revenue

Enterprise

For organisations where Java is critical and who want to actively support and evolve Jakarta EE.

$10k – $50k USD
based on annual revenue

Participant

For organisations using or building on Jakarta EE and staying engaged in the ecosystem.

$5k – $20k USD
based on annual revenue
